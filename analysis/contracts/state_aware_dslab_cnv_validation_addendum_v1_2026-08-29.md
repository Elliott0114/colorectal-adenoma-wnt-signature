# Implementation addendum: self-generated CNV-composition validation of the frozen state-shared programme (v1)

**Date:** 29 August 2026
**Status:** FROZEN before calculating any 1,843-gene or eight-gene outcome in the DSLab series
**Parent contract:** `state_aware_program_rederivation_v1_2026-08-29.md`
**Scope:** orthogonal composition sensitivity and compact-readout fidelity; no gene selection, state-label transfer or clinical classification

## Information known at freeze

- The state-aware discovery analysis fixed a strict programme of 1,843 genes (884 up and 959 down).
- The discovery-only compact reconstruction fixed eight genes in four balanced up/down pairs before held-out validation.
- The existing DSLab source audit identified 42,574 source-confirmed epithelial cells from 15 polyps, including eight conventional adenomas, and fixed patient tokens, CNV values and pathology groupings.
- Prior analyses of the older 287- and 12-gene sets indicated that CNV composition explained little patient-to-patient score variation, but no outcome for the new 1,843- or eight-gene definitions has been calculated.
- Programme-blind fine-state transfer failed its public held-out accuracy check and remains prohibited.

## Frozen inputs

1. Strict state-shared membership and direction come from `results/state_aware_program_v1/common_effects/cross_state_common_effects.tsv.gz`.
2. Compact membership and direction come from `results/state_aware_program_v1/panel_derivation/compact_state_shared_panel_frozen.tsv`.
3. Cell inclusion, patient tokenisation, CNV linkage and pathology groupings reuse the audited DSLab v1 inputs without modification.
4. Source patient identifiers and cell barcodes may be used transiently for local linkage but must never be written to candidate outputs.

## Population and scores

- Source-confirmed `Polyp_Epi` cells form the analysis population.
- CNV-high status uses the existing sample-specific rule `cnv_fraction >= cnv_threshold`; other cells with numeric CNV values are CNV-low.
- A patient contributes to paired CNV analysis only if both compartments contain at least 50 cells.
- For cross-platform robustness, each cell is ranked across measurable frozen genes and converted to percentile ranks. The score is mean up-arm percentile minus mean down-arm percentile, with equal arm weighting.
- The full score uses every measurable member of the frozen 1,843-gene programme. The compact score uses the frozen four-up/four-down readout. No gene is substituted, reweighted or removed after outcomes are inspected.

## Primary and secondary analyses

The primary population is the eight conventional adenomas.

1. Compare paired CNV-high and CNV-low full-programme means using an exact two-sided Wilcoxon test.
2. Recalculate each patient at the equal-patient mean CNV-high fraction and quantify preservation of the observed patient ordering by Spearman correlation with a 5,000 whole-patient bootstrap interval.
3. Quantify the range generated by CNV composition alone relative to the observed patient range.
4. Use the existing exact Oaxaca-style expansion and equal-split Shapley allocation to describe composition and within-compartment contributions. These are accounting quantities, not causal mediation effects.
5. Test compact-readout fidelity as the Spearman correlation between compact and full common-composition patient scores.

The ten adenomatous polyps and all 15 polyps are population sensitivities. Pathology-group separation is retained only as a descriptive negative-control audit and cannot be used to claim diagnostic performance.

## Interpretation boundary

- A positive result supports resistance of patient ordering to CNV-composition standardisation and/or measurement fidelity of the compact readout in a self-generated series.
- It does not validate adenoma-versus-normal discrimination, a clinical threshold, prognosis, intervention response, a pharmacodynamic biomarker or fine epithelial-state transfer.
- Null or discordant outcomes must be reported and cannot trigger gene or score revision.
