# Epithelial-state decomposition v1 — result summary

## Primary held-out result

- Donor-balanced conventional-adenoma minus normal 287-gene score: 0.556 (0.377 to 0.727).
- Composition component: 0.229 (0.137 to 0.295).
- Within-state component: 0.327 (0.221 to 0.451).
- Observed within-state share of the signed total: 58.9%. This ratio is descriptive because bootstrap ratios become unstable when a resampled total approaches zero.
- Exact numerical closure error: 0.000e+00.

## Prespecified robustness checks

- Canonical seven-state within-state component: 0.379 (0.261 to 0.501).
- Frozen 12-gene within-state component: 0.612 (0.410 to 0.849).
- Donor-route 287-gene and 12-gene scores were concordant in held-out normal/conventional samples (Spearman rho=0.966, n=32).
- Discovery-partition 287-gene within-state component: 0.312 (0.220 to 0.405) (selection-aware replication).

## Interpretation rule

The analysis distinguishes an accounting contribution from altered epithelial-state abundance and an accounting contribution from different scores within deposited states. It does not establish causality, lineage conversion or unbiased in situ cell abundance.
